Lasagna

Description

Recipe for Gluten Free Lasagna Dish

Quick and simple midweek meal. Great accompanied with a salad a slice or two of garlic bread.

Ingredients

  • Gluten Free Past Sheets
  • 500g Mince Meat or Substitute
  • 500g Tomato Passata
  • Table Spoon of Tomato Paste
  • Teaspoon of Salt
  • 2 Table Spoons of Sugar
  • Favourite Herbs, Spices and Sauces
  • Litter of Milk
    • Lactose Free Milk if Preferred
  • 250g of Preferred Cheese
    • Lactose Free Cheese if Preferred
  • 50g of Butter
  • 50g of Cornflour

Steps

  1. Fry Mince
  2. Add Tomato Passata, Paste, Salt, Sugar and Herbs and Spices
  3. Add 500 ml of Water
  4. Bring to the boil and simmer until thickened
  5. Add milk and butter to stove
  6. Slowly bring to simmer
  7. Dilute Cornflour in 50ml of Cold Milk
  8. Slowly Add diluted Cornflour to heated milk and slowly stir threw
  9. Bring to simmer and ensure milk thickened
  10. Turn off heat and add cheese to milk
  11. Stir Cheese threw
  12. Layer one third of mince in pan
  13. Add one layer of Lasagna sheets
  14. Add layer of cheese sauce
  15. Repeat Process two more
  16. Cook as per Instructions for Lasagna Sheets
