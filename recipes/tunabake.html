Tuna Bake

Description

Recipe for Gluten Free Tuna Bake

Quick and simple midweek meal. Great accompanied with a salad or a slice or two of garlic bread.

Ingredients

  • 300g Gluten Free Pasta
  • Tin of Tuna (Brined or Spring Water)
  • 200g of Sweetcorn
  • Half Teaspoon of Salt
  • Litter of Milk
    • Lactose Free Milk if Preferred
  • 250g of Preferred Cheese
    • Lactose Free Cheese if Preferred
  • 50g of Butter
  • 50g of Cornflour

Steps

  1. Cook Pasta as Per Packet Instructions and Drain
  2. Add milk and butter to stove
  3. Slowly bring to simmer
  4. Dilute Cornflour in 50ml of Cold Milk
  5. Slowly Add diluted Cornflour to heated milk and slowly stir threw
  6. Bring to simmer and ensure milk thickened
  7. Turn off heat and add cheese to milk
  8. Stir Cheese threw
  9. Mix Sweetcorn, Pasta and Tuna with a Quarter of the Cheese Sauce
  10. Layer at Bottom of Pan
  11. Layer Remaining Cheese Sauce on Top
  12. Cook in middle of Oven for 45m at Gas Mark 6
