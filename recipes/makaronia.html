Makaronia du Furnu

Description

Recipe for Gluten Free Makaronia du Furnu

Quick and simple midweek meal. Great accompanied with a salad or a slice or two of garlic bread.

Ingredients

  • Gluten Free Pasta
    • Preferrably Long Tubes or Penne
  • 500g Mince Meat or Substitute
  • Teaspoon of Salt
  • Favourite Herbs, Spices and Sauces
  • Litter of Milk
    • Lactose Free Milk if Preferred
  • 250g of Preferred Cheese
    • Lactose Free Cheese if Preferred
  • 50g of Butter
  • 50g of Cornflour
  • Two Eggs

Steps

  1. Fry Mince
  2. Add Herbs and Spices
  3. Add 150 ml of Water
  4. Bring to the boil and simmer until thickened
  5. Add milk and butter to stove
  6. Slowly bring to simmer
  7. Dilute Cornflour in 50ml of Cold Milk
  8. Slowly Add diluted Cornflour to heated milk and slowly stir threw
  9. Bring to simmer and ensure milk thickened
  10. Turn off heat and add cheese to milk
  11. Stir Cheese threw
  12. Add bitten eggs
  13. Layer Pasta in Dish
  14. Add a third of Cheese sauce and Stir threw
  15. Layer Mince on top of Pasta
  16. Layer Remaining Cheese Sauce on top
  17. Cook in Center of Oven for 45 minutes at Gas Mark 5
